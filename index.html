01Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes has the strongest evidence. Remission is a measurable state: HbA1c below 6.5% without medication.

Study / PersonnMethodResultEvidence
DiRECTTaylor, Lancet 2018 · 1 yr · 2 yr · 5 yr 298 825–853 kcal/day, 3–5 months, meds stopped first Remission 46% vs. 4% (1 yr), 36% (2 yr), 13% (5 yr, 11/85 extension). From 15 kg loss: 86%.
Simply: half of people were no longer diabetic after one year, just from a short liquid diet and losing weight. The more weight lost, the higher the chance.
 RCT · strongest
Virta HealthHallberg · 1 yr · 2 yr · 5 yr 262 Keto + physician-managed de-prescribing 1 yr: HbA1c 7.6 → 6.3%, 13.8 kg, 94% insulin reduced/stopped. 2 yr: insulin dose 81.9 → 15.5 U/day. 5 yr: 20% remission, −7.6%.
Simply: blood sugar fell from diabetic to near-normal, people lost ~14 kg, and almost everyone on insulin could cut or stop it.
 open-label, non-randomized
WestmanDuke, 2008 · PubMed 84 Keto <20 g vs. low-GI, 24 weeks HbA1c −1.5 vs. −0.5%. Meds reduced/off in 95% vs. 62%.
Simply: cutting carbs under 20 g worked about twice as well as a standard diet for lowering blood sugar, and more people could drop their diabetes pills.
 RCT
UnwinGP practice, 2020 · PubMed 128 Low carb, 23 months HbA1c 8.1 → 6.5%. 46% drug-free remission.
Simply: in one real family-doctor practice, nearly half of patients got their diabetes into remission just from low-carb advice — no drugs.
 practice data
Jason FungBMJ Case Rep 2018 · PubMed 3 24-h fasting, 3×/week Insulin stopped after 5/13/18 days, HbA1c 11 → 7, 7.2 → 6, 6.8 → 6.2.
Simply: three men stopped insulin entirely within days of fasting and their blood sugar stayed under control.
 case series
BikmanWalton, 2019 · PubMed 11 Keto <30 g, 90 days HbA1c 8.9 → 5.6% (women only).
Simply: eleven women went from clearly diabetic (8.9%) to a normal blood sugar (5.6%) in 90 days of keto.
 small series
LustigObesity 2016 · PubMed 43 Sugar 28 → 10% of calories, 9 days (children) Triglycerides −46%, BP −5 mmHg, hyperinsulinemia improved. No remission data.
Simply: in children, swapping sugar for starch cut blood fats by almost half and improved blood pressure — without any weight loss.
 before/after
Carnivore surveyLennerz, 2021 · PubMed 2,029 Meat-only, self-reported 262 diabetics: medication reduction 84–100%.
Simply: people who ate only meat said they cut way down on diabetes meds — but it's self-reported and unverified.
 self-reported

No own evidence: Casey Means. Negative: Cleveland Clinic functional medicine RCT (n=71, PubMed) — primary endpoint 15.8% vs. 11.1%, p=0.68. Simply: functional medicine made no measurable difference.

02Cancer

Much thinner evidence: case reports, preclinical, small phase-2 trials. Nothing replaces standard therapy.

Study / PersonnMethodResultEvidence
LongoDIRECT, Nat Commun 2020 · PubMed 131 Fasting-mimicking diet + chemo (breast cancer) No toxicity difference despite omitting dexamethasone. Radiological response OR 3.17, pathological OR 4.11. Only ~20% completed all cycles.
Simply: fasting around chemo didn't add side effects, and tumors shrank more often (about 3× the odds). But most people couldn't stick to it.
 phase-2 RCT
SeyfriedKeto cases, DOAC 3 Keto, fasting, glucose-lowering agents 3 glioblastoma cases. One lived 80 months (32 y, IDH1-mutant). One tumor-free on PET after 2 months, then relapse.
Simply: a few individual brain-tumor patients lived longer on keto — but single cases, some with tumors that run slow anyway.
 single cases
D'AgostinoJRE #994, #1176 — Keto + hyperbaric oxygen Mice: survival +57% (keto), +78% (with oxygen). No human data.
Simply: mice lived longer on keto — nothing proven in humans yet.
 animal study
Colin ChampJ Neurooncol 2014 · PubMed 6 Keto during radiotherapy (glioma) Glucose 84 vs. 122 mg/dL, 4/6 alive after 14 months.
Simply: six brain-tumor patients kept lower blood sugar during radiation; 4 of 6 were alive after 14 months.
 mini series
Khodabakhshi 80 Keto, mixed tumors Shrinkage 27 vs. 6 mm, but no benefit in metastatic disease.
Simply: tumors shrank more on keto (27 vs 6 mm), but that didn't help once cancer had already spread.
 small study
William LiDOAC — Anti-angiogenesis foods Prevention plausible, treatment claims only cell/animal data.
Simply: eating "blood-vessel-starving" foods is a plausible prevention idea, but the treatment claims only come from cells and animals.
 preclinical

Sobering: ERGO2, KETOCOMP, metformin in breast cancer (n=3,649, HR 1.01) — controlled trials mostly showed no extra benefit. Self-reported without records: Jane McLelland, Nasha Winters. Single case: Raffi Kalamian (brain tumor 2004, standard therapy failed → keto, catalyst for Miriam Kalamian's work).

03Heart

Plaque regression is possible, best documented with medication and the Mediterranean diet.

Study / PersonnMethodResultEvidence
OrnishJAMA 1990 / 1998 · PubMed 48 Low-fat vegetarian + exercise + stress + group After 5 years stenosis −3.1 vs. +11.8 points. 25 vs. 45 events.
Simply: with diet, exercise, stress work and a group, arteries actually opened slightly after 5 years — and people had far fewer heart events.
 small RCT
Esselstyn1999 / J Fam Pract 2014 · PubMed 198 Plant-based, no oil + lipid-lowering drugs Adherent (177): 1 stroke in 3.7 years (0.6%). Non-adherent: 13/21 (62%).
Simply: those who stuck to the strict plant diet almost never had a stroke or heart event; those who didn't, did.
 uncontrolled
Attia / ApoBGLAGOV · EVAPORATE 968 / 80 ApoB lowering (medication) GLAGOV: plaque −0.95% vs. +0.05%. EVAPORATE: −17% vs. +109% soft plaque.
Simply: lowering "bad" cholesterol (ApoB) with drugs shrank plaque in the arteries.
 RCT
Mediterranean dietLyon · CORDIOPREV · PREDIMED ~1,000+ Mediterranean CORDIOPREV: events HR 0.72–0.75. PREDIMED: HR 0.69/0.72.
Simply: eating Mediterranean cut heart events by roughly a quarter to a third.
 RCT
Goldhamer2001 · PubMed + 2024 · PubMed 174 / 29 Water-only fasting, inpatient (hypertension) ~90% below 140/90, mean −37/−13 mmHg. 2024: n=29, below 130/80, all meds discontinued.
Simply: water fasting dropped blood pressure a lot — most people got off their blood-pressure pills.
 uncontrolled

Counter-example: Look AHEAD (5,145 diabetics, 9.6 years lifestyle) — weight loss alone didn't prevent heart events. No reversal studies: William Davis, Steven Gundry (0 nutrition publications, AHA expression of concern 2021), Malhotra, Kahn. KETO-CTA retracted 2026. Nothing solid on heart failure.

09Why it works

The mechanism is understood and called the twin cycle (Roy Taylor): calorie surplus stores fat in the liver and pancreas, the beta cells reduce insulin production.

Losing about 15 kg de-fats both organs — insulin production recovers in many. That's why the method barely matters: it's the weight loss, not "keto" or "fasting" itself. Not everyone reaches remission; the longer the disease, the less likely.

Honest verdict: real, reproducible — but no miracle.

Diabetes remission through weight loss is mechanistically explained and achieves the same results as surgery or GLP-1. In cancer the evidence only reaches case reports and small phase-2 trials — nothing replaces standard therapy. In heart disease plaque regression is best documented with medication and the Mediterranean diet. What's remarkable is not the biology, but that remission as a goal is underused. Whoever sells it as a revolutionary single solution and adds supplements is telling only half the story.
