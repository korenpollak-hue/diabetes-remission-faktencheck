Counter-example: Look AHEAD (5,145 diabetics, 9.6 years lifestyle) — no reduction in cardiac events. No reversal studies: William Davis (claims published data, no peer-reviewed case series found), Steven Gundry (0 nutrition publications, AHA expression of concern 2021), Malhotra, Kahn. KETO-CTA retracted 2026. Nothing solid on heart failure.

Mind-body works mainly additively: Ornish's RCT is the only program that combined diet + exercise + stress + group. Meditation, sleep and stress reduction lower blood pressure and inflammation and improve adherence — they do not replace the calorie deficit.

06The podcast layer has a problem

The starting point of this research was a podcast — and that very source is documented as problematic.

  • BBC (Dec 2024): More than 20 health episodes of the Diary of a CEO analyzed — on average 14 contradicting health claims per episode. Explicitly named: Seyfried's keto-for-cancer episode. Experts: David Grimes, Heidi Larson, Partha Kar, Liz O'Riordan.
  • Gary Brecka: Dana White case without lab values, no concrete case numbers on DOAC, "44% MTHFR" claim without clinical context. Science Feedback (July 2024, Mayo Clinic professors) rated his Hashimoto's claims "Inaccurate".

10Why it works

The mechanism is understood and called the twin cycle (Roy Taylor): calorie surplus stores fat in the liver and pancreas, the beta cells reduce insulin production.

Losing about 15 kg de-fats both organs — insulin production recovers in many. That's why the method barely matters: it's the weight loss, not "keto" or "fasting" itself. Not everyone reaches remission; the longer the disease, the less likely.

Honest verdict: real, reproducible — but no miracle.

Diabetes remission through weight loss is mechanistically explained and achieves the same results as surgery or GLP-1. In cancer the evidence only reaches case reports and small phase-2 trials — nothing replaces standard therapy. In heart disease plaque regression is best documented with medication and the Mediterranean diet. What's remarkable is not the biology, but that remission as a goal is underused. Whoever sells it as a revolutionary single solution and adds supplements is telling only half the story.
